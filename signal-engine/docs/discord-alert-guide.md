# How FateDrop alerts work

FateDrop is designed to avoid the usual "everything is important" stock-alert noise.

We monitor broadly, but every alert means something different. The four FateDrop signals describe how strong the evidence is and what you should actually do with it.

**Not every product will go through all four stages.** FateDrop only sends a stage when the evidence supports it.

---

## 🔹 Oru · WHISPER
### Something moved.

Whisper is FateDrop's early product-intelligence signal.

It can mean a new SKU has appeared, a product has entered a retailer catalogue, or a product changed state before stock was verified.

**What it means:** something may be developing.

**What it does NOT mean:** stock is live.

**What to do:** keep an eye on it, inspect the product, or create a FateFind.

---

## 🟣 Fenn · ECHO
### The retailer is waking up.

Echo watches retailer readiness rather than simply watching product listings.

It can be triggered by meaningful queue, traffic, access or security behaviour around a retailer.

**What it means:** conditions around the retailer have changed and it may be worth getting ready.

**What it does NOT mean:** stock is confirmed.

**What to do:** get ready and inspect — but don't treat an Echo as a guaranteed drop.

---

## 🟢 Koru · MANIFESTED
### It's live.

Manifested is FateDrop's confirmed availability alert.

FateDrop has observed the offer as genuinely purchasable.

**What it means:** verified stock is available now.

**What to do:** if you want it, this is the alert to act on.

For independent/market sellers, FateDrop can also show price intelligence so you can judge the offer against RRP and other sellers rather than buying blindly.

---

## 🔴 Nyxen · VANISHED
### It's gone.

Vanished means stock that FateDrop previously verified as purchasable is no longer available.

Where FateDrop has a complete observation window, Vanished can also show:

**Observed live · 12m 34s**

That is the measured time between FateDrop seeing the product become live and seeing it disappear.

**What to do:** check alternatives, compare other sellers, or keep the hunt active for the next restock.

---

# The simple version

**Whisper** → "Something changed."

**Echo** → "The retailer looks active. Get ready."

**Manifested** → "Verified stock is live."

**Vanished** → "Verified stock has gone."

# Why FateDrop does it this way

A lot of alert services simply detect a page change and fire a notification.

FateDrop tries to understand **what changed, how reliable it is, and whether it deserves to interrupt you**.

That is also why you can optimise your own alerts. FateDrop can keep monitoring accessories, merchandise and other network activity without forcing you to receive every one of those notifications.

The goal is simple:

**Monitor everything. Understand it. Interrupt selectively.**
